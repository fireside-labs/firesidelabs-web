Fireside Voice

Privacy Policy

Fireside Voice translates on your phone. We do not collect, store or see anything you say.

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version. The app has no account, no analytics and no advertising. Your voice and your words stay on your device. We run no server that receives them.

What stays on your phone

  • Your voice. The microphone is used to turn speech into text on the device. Audio is not saved and is never sent anywhere.
  • Your conversations. Text and translations exist on screen while a conversation is open. They are cleared when you end it. If you use Share or Copy, the text goes only where you send it.
  • Your settings. Your name, your languages and your preferences are stored on the device.

Tables: phones talking to each other

When you host or join a table, the phones at that table connect to each other directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. No internet connection or server is involved. The phones exchange the name and language you chose and the text of what is said at the table, so each person can read it in their own language. That connection is encrypted. Audio is not sent between phones.

The one time the app uses the internet

On first setup, and when you add a language, the app downloads speech and translation models. These files come from public file hosts (Hugging Face and GitHub). As with any download, the host sees your device's internet address and the file requested. The app sends them nothing else. After the download, every feature works with no connection.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone and speech recognition. To hear what is said and turn it into text on the device.
  • Local network and Bluetooth. To connect phones at a table.
  • Camera. Only to scan another phone's code when you join a table. No photos or video are captured.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising tools.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we have none.

Using the app around other people

Fireside Voice shows the words of people speaking near the phone. Please use it where the people speaking are comfortable with that, and follow the rules that apply where you are.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
