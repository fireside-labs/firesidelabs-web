Frequently asked

Does it really work with no internet?

Yes. The first setup downloads about 4 GB of language models, so do that on Wi-Fi. After that, every feature works in airplane mode.

The download stopped or failed. What do I do?

Check that you are on Wi-Fi and have enough free space for a 4 GB download, then tap Try again. The download picks up where it left off. If you only have mobile data, you can allow it from the download screen.

Which way should I use it?

One phone, shared is for two people passing one phone. Everyone's own phone is for a group: each person joins a table and reads in their own language. Just listen is for a talk, a service or a tour, where one person speaks and you read along.

How do we start a table?

One person chooses Everyone's own phone and starts a table. The others scan the code on that screen, or type the room code. Phones need to be in the same room. No Wi-Fi network is required.

Someone has an older phone. Can they join?

Yes. A phone that is too small to run the translator can still join a table. A newer phone at the table translates for it, so at least one newer phone needs to be there.

I see the words but hear nothing.

That is how the app starts. Translations are shown as text. To have them spoken aloud, tap the speaker button during a conversation.

Just listen is missing words.

Move closer to the person speaking, or point the bottom of the phone toward them. It works best with one clear voice. Music, singing and several people talking at once are hard for it.

It stopped when I locked my phone.

The phone cannot translate while it is locked. The app keeps the screen on by itself during a conversation. If you lock it, it pauses and catches up when you come back.

Which languages work best?

English and Polish are the most tested. Other languages are included and newer, and may be rougher. Tell us what you find.

Is my conversation saved anywhere?

No. Nothing leaves your phone, and the text is cleared when you end a conversation. See the Privacy Policy.
