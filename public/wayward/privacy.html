Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026

Wayward helps you track the Schengen 90/180-day rule. Your travel data is yours. We built the app to work entirely on your device, with cloud sync only if you choose to sign in — and we never sell your data, run ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

Wayward is operated by Fireside Labs LLC, 455 E Continental Dr, Tempe, AZ 85288-1056, United States. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at support@firesidelabs.ai.

The short version

  • Works offline. You can use Wayward fully without an account. Trips are stored on your device.
  • Sign-in is optional. Create an account only if you want cloud backup, sync across devices, or Couples mode.
  • No ads. No trackers. No analytics SDKs. We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook, Sentry, or any third-party tracking.
  • We never sell your data and never share it for advertising.
  • Smart Scan runs on your device. It reads photo dates and locations locally to suggest trips — your photos are never uploaded to us.

Signing in

If you choose to sign in, you can use Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google, or an email magic link. We receive an email address and an account identifier so we can sync your trips and enable Couples mode. Sign in with Apple lets you hide your email with Apple's private relay if you prefer. You can delete your account at any time (see below).

Smart Scan & photos

If you use Smart Scan, Wayward asks permission to read your photo library. It looks only at each photo's metadata — the date taken and, where available, the location — to detect international trips and suggest them for you to confirm. This analysis happens entirely on your device. We do not upload, copy, or store your photos. You can decline photo access and log trips manually instead.

How your data is protected

Cloud data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on secured infrastructure with access controls that restrict your trips to your account (and your partner's, in Couples mode). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your choices & rights

  • Use without an account — no personal data leaves your device.
  • Access & export — export a PDF record of your trips from within the app.
  • Delete your account & cloud data — email support@firesidelabs.ai and we will delete your account and associated cloud data.
  • Disconnect a couple — either partner can end the connection at any time.
  • Revoke permissions — turn off photo access or notifications anytime in iOS Settings.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. We honor these requests for all users — just contact us.

Children

Wayward is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
