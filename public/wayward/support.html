Frequently asked

How does the 90/180 rule work?

The Schengen short-stay rule lets you spend up to 90 days within any rolling 180-day period. The 180-day window moves forward every day, so days you spent more than 180 days ago no longer count. Wayward does this math for you and shows exactly how many days you have left and when they return.

Do I need an account?

No. Wayward works fully offline and stores your trips on your device. Create an optional account only if you want cloud backup, sync across devices, or Couples mode.

What is Smart Scan?

Smart Scan reads the dates and locations saved in your photos — on your device only — to suggest international trips you can confirm with a tap. Your photos are never uploaded. You can also log trips manually.

How does Couples mode work?

Connect with your partner to share one joint 90/180 window and see both of your days in one view. Either partner can disconnect at any time.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name → Subscriptions → Wayward, and you can change or cancel there. Refunds are handled by Apple.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Wayward, go to the Premium screen, and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email support@firesidelabs.ai from your account email and we'll delete your account and any cloud data associated with it.

Is Wayward legal advice?

No. Wayward is a planning tool to help you stay organized. Always confirm entry and stay rules with official government sources before you travel.
